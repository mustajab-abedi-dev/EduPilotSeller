😰 The Pain

Right now, your school runs on WhatsApp, spreadsheets, and guessing.

Every Indian principal knows the chaos. Let's name it.

🔍

No Single Source of Truth

Principals must call HODs, check WhatsApp groups, and chase spreadsheets to know which classes are behind or which departments are late on reports.

🚨

Inspection Panic

Scrambling for files, lesson plans, and compliance reports days before CBSE inspections or internal audits. Every time feels like a fire drill.

😩

Teacher Overload

Teachers spending late evenings on remarks, grading, and approvals instead of focused teaching. Manual report cards take weeks every term.

👁️

Missed Student Issues

Attendance concerns, academic struggles, and high performers only noticed when it's too late — end of term, parent complaints, or board exam results.
